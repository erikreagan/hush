Hush cleans up your mic before any app hears it.

A Mac menu bar app that takes the fans, typing and clicks out of your microphone, then hands every app the clean version.

Get the source on GitHub Free and open source. You build it yourself.

How it works

  1. It listens to your real microphone. Any input your Mac can see.
  2. It removes the background noise. Fan hum, keyboard typing, mouse clicks and desk thuds, processed on your Mac.
  3. It feeds a virtual microphone called Hush Mic. Zoom, Meet, Slack, browsers and recording apps pick Hush Mic as their input, or use it automatically because Hush sets it as the Mac's default.

What you get

  • Two noise-removal enginesSwitch between Apple's built-in voice isolation and DeepFilterNet 3.
  • Three strength levelsPick how much noise gets taken out.
  • Hold to compareHear the raw mic against the cleaned one, or monitor yourself with Listen.
  • Hard muteSilence to every app at once.
  • Global keyboard shortcutsControl it without opening the menu.
  • Stream Deck pluginToggle, strength, mute and a live level meter on an Elgato Stream Deck.
  • Command line toolhush-cli runs the same engines over a WAV file for A/B comparisons.
  • Only your micNo recording, transcription or meeting notes. Mic in, clean mic out.

Get it

Hush was built for one person's desk and is shared as-is. There is no installer or signed release. You need an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later, the Xcode command line tools, and Rust.

git clone --recursive https://github.com/erikreagan/hush.git
cd hush

scripts/install-driver.sh   # installs the virtual mic; needs admin
scripts/build-app.sh        # builds build/Hush.app
open build/Hush.app

Full instructions, including the Stream Deck plugin, are in the README.
